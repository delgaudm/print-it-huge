# Gallery candidate images

The sample gallery for Print It Huge — public-domain images chosen to show off large-format
printing: bold silhouettes, dense detail, flat graphic color, and a mix of orientations and
aspect ratios. `manifest.json` holds the same data in structured form (titles, artists,
licenses, groups, source pages, dimensions) and drives the in-app gallery modal.

All images are public domain or CC0. Faithful photographic scans of 2-D public domain works
do not create a new copyright (US: *Bridgeman v. Corel*; Wikimedia Commons treats them as PD),
so printing these is safe. Keep the source-page links for credit display.

`thumbs/` holds 440px JPEG thumbnails for the gallery grid (generated by
`scripts/prep-gallery.sh` — re-run it after adding or replacing images). Full images are only
downloaded when a user picks one.

## Known nits

- **17-beach-scene**: attribution not yet verified (Alfred Stevens vs. Eugène Boudin style);
  the rawpixel watermark was removed by cropping (image is 1688×861 of the original 2047×945).
- **16-mondrian-composition**: rawpixel watermark removed by cropping (1884×1877 of 2048×2041).
- **14-cosmic-cliffs** is 1920px — fine for dot/dither styles; images.nasa.gov has a 14,575px
  original if we want a full-res hero image.
- **03-girl-pearl-earring** (1861px) and **07-sierra-nevada** (2599px) are workable but have
  bigger scans available on their source pages.

## Why these images enlarge well

1. **Source resolution** — most are 2500–5100px on the long edge, enough for ~150 DPI at poster size.
2. **Bold value structure** — Great Wave, Wanderer, Starry Night read instantly from across the room,
   and survive halftone/dither rendering.
3. **Detail density** — Seurat's dots, Haeckel's linework, Audubon's feather detail reward close viewing.
4. **Flat graphic color** — ukiyo-e, Mucha, Mondrian, and Morris were designed as flat-ink images.
5. **Format variety** — square (Klimt, Mondrian), tall portrait (Mucha, Audubon, Curtis, Haeckel),
   wide (Venus, Grande Jatte), ultra-wide (beach scene, cosmic cliffs) — exercises every tiling layout.
